Privacy Policy

Plaintxt · Last updated 8 September 2026

Plaintxt is a text editor. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing about you is collected, stored or sold by us. The app never asks who you are.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Plaintxt has no network features. Files are read from and written to the location you choose, and stay there.

What is kept

Only the files you edit, wherever you saved them. Plaintxt keeps no copies, no history, and no settings on any server.

Third parties

None. On the Mac, the optional completion feature talks only to a model running on your own computer.

Children

Plaintxt does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above changes with it.
